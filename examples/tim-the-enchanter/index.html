Tim the Enchanter

Gallagher the cat explores a dungeon room, one flagstone at a time, behind the chairs and up onto them. A 2018 prototype, rebuilt on the current Tenkai engine.

Walk into a chair to hop onto it, or find a way out.

Controls

KeyWalkingMap editor
ArrowsWalk; hold to keep goingMove the cursor, or click a cell
TabOpen the editorBack to walking
Q EPrevious or next tile layer
A D W SStep the cell's tile through the tileset
BackspaceClear the layer in this cell
O EnterChoose a piece of furniture; place it at the cursor
BForce a cell solid or open, or back to what its tiles say
Z XKeep the map in this browser; revert to the original
