# Origin

Tenkai was first written to build a Suikoden II themed spinoff game, a birthday present for a member of the
Suikoden Revival Movement.

The name comes from the Tenkai Star (天魁星), the Star of Destiny that belongs to the hero in the Suikoden
series and leads the 108 Stars. The logo follows the same thread: a gate, a sun and mountains, drawn in the
spirit of the games.

## A note on intent

Tenkai, its name and its logo are meant as a homage to Suikoden II, a truly great piece of Japanese digital
art. The creator of Tenkai is not Japanese, and has tried to use these references with care and respect.

If you find anything in Tenkai's name, logo, documentation or examples culturally inappropriate or
otherwise problematic, please contact me directly at tomhughcully@gmail.com. I would much rather hear about
it and put it right.
